FOR CHELLE · 2023

EPISODE 01 · THE THINGS I WANTED TO SAY

Dear Chelle

Hello Chelle!

I want to start by saying thank you very much for everything. Thank you for always being by my side, and for the support you have given me.

When we met, I'm not really sure if we would get along. Super small interaction lang natin pag nagkikita tayo, and all of a sudden, biglang naging close tayo at instantly nag-click yung humor natin noong 2023. And honestly, I'm glad na naging close tayo kasi nagkaroon ako ng matatawag kong ATE. When life is tough, you're always beside me.

Simula nung naging close tayo, medyo naging inseparable tayo. Pag may family events and outings, tayong tatlo yung magkakasama at nagtatawanan na parang may sariling mundo. Thank you for the memories that are worth cherishing, thank you for the random talks, laughter, at tsimisan na hinding-hindi ko makakalimutan.

Looking back, ang dami na pala nating talagang napagsamahan. From walang masyadong kibuan to closeness overload with sleepovers. From random na chika lang to juicy na tsismis. From casual talking to deep conversations and rants about life na super special sa'kin kasi I can feel na you trust me enough para sabihin mo sa'kin ang mga iyan.

I care about you, and I can say na you care for me too kasi kahit hindi ko sabihin sa'yo na hindi ako okay, somehow you still know when I need someone to talk to. Sobrang galing mo mag-observe ng mga bagay, kaya I'm really thankful na nandiyan ka para malabas ko yung mga thoughts ko na akala ko ay walang makakaintindi.

Thank you, Chelle—thank you for always backing me up, thank you sa pagtanggap mo sa kung sino at anuman ako, thank you for always staying, and thank you for everything you've done and said to me. I always appreciate the small and big efforts you've made just to comfort me.

I know you're going through a hard time now, and I wish na malampasan mo rin 'yan. If anything or everything gets out of control, please remember I'm always here to help you and will always support you!

For all of the things you did, I'm really, really, reallyyyy thankful.

You deserve all the nice things that came and will come into your life!

You deserve a sunshine, Chelle! ☀️

I LOVE YOU! 💗
